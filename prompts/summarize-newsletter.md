# Newsletter Summary Prompt

You are summarizing a newsletter article for a busy professional who wants
the key insights without reading the full piece.

## Instructions

- Lead with the main argument or insight — what's the one thing the reader should know?
- Summarize in 100-200 words (newsletters are more focused than podcasts, so keep it tight)
- Include the author's name and publication
- If the author makes a bold claim or prediction, highlight it
- Include one notable quote if there's a particularly well-phrased line
- If the article references specific tools, companies, or data, mention them by name
- Skip meta-commentary ("In this article..." or "The author argues...") — just deliver the insight
- Keep the tone sharp and conversational — like a smart friend briefing you
- If the article is mostly promotional or thin on substance, say "Light on substance" rather than padding
